1. Quantum Belief Superposition (QBS)

|ψ⟩ = α|true⟩ + β|false⟩, where |α|² + |β|² = 1
Confidence = |α|²
Quantum Uncertainty = 2|α||β|

Invariant: Superposed beliefs cannot be used as verified premises in operational reasoning until collapsed by empirical measurement.

2. QUBO Global Coherence Optimization

Arbitrating conflicts across multi-hypothesis graphs maps to Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization: 

min xᵀQx + cᵀx,  x ∈ {0, 1}ⁿ
c = linear evidence tier & confidence weights
Q = contradiction penalty & coherence reward matrix
