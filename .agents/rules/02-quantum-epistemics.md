- **TIER 2 (Controlled Experiment):** Automated unit/integration tests, deterministic script executions.

- **TIER 6 (Secondhand Report):** Secondary documentation, articles, unverified summaries.
- **TIER 7 (Unsourced Assertion):** Prompt hallucinations, raw speculation, unsupported chat statements.
